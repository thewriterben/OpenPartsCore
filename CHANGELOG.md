# Changelog

## [Unreleased]
### Added
- **ADR-0007: `features` - where things are on a part, for one revision, with its own source** (2026-09-26). OpenDesignCore cut two camera mounts for `boards/lilygo-t-camera-plus-s3` from an outline and neither could be used: v0.1 hit the JST connectors, v0.2 walled off the USB-C port. The vendor STEP knew where both were; the registry kept only its bounding box. New optional top-level `features {source{citation, model_sha256, revision}, frame, not_modelled[], items[]}`; each item is a box or an axis-aligned cylinder with a `source`, and anything a plug, card or finger must reach carries `access {direction, depth_mm}` in which **null means unknown** and absence is invalid. `validate.py` enforces it, and now also enforces the schema's top-level `additionalProperties:false`, which it never had; `scripts/test_validate.py` (12 cases, in CI) pins the rules. `features` joins the ingest's `PRESERVED_KEYS`; the TS binding types it; the Rust binding does not hoist it, because nothing reads it there yet.
- **`scripts/extract_step_features.py`**: per-component census of a vendor STEP (OCCT via `uv run --with cadquery-ocp==7.9.3.1.1`, by hand, not CI), one box per placed component with its bodies unioned, byte-identical on repeat; `--items` emits a draft `features.items` with every access unknown.
- **First features: `boards/lilygo-t-camera-plus-s3` at V1.0-V1.1**, 146 items from LILYGO's V1.0-V1.1 STEP, which was not previously measured (downloaded 2026-09-26, sha256 `9f8e6d6c...82ab9`). V1.1 because that is the bench board; against V1.2 the laminate is 1.20 vs 1.14 mm, CN4 and LED1 sit about 1 mm closer to the USB edge, and V1.2 adds a slide switch V1.1 lacks, while the bottom-edge connectors are identical. USB1, CN1 and CN2 overhang the laminate edge by 1.6 mm; their access direction is recorded as **inferred** from that overhang, and every depth is unknown. `not_modelled` says what the models omit: the camera module and lens, the display, the attached cables.

### Changed
- **The envelope note's "lens-barrel shape" was SW3**, the 6 x 6 mm tactile switch - named by the census. Neither vendor model contains the camera module or the display, so the envelope is the populated PCB only; a V1.1 board as fitted measures 29.40 mm deep. The note now says so. The envelope's numbers are unchanged.
- **The LILYGO OV5640 question is now one measurement, not a disassembly** (2026-09-12). The entry has said since it landed that the model's camera module is unidentified and the OV5640 SKU's height therefore unverified — true, and useless to act on. A census of all **825 solids** in the STEP says where z actually comes from: the top face is a single solid **6.2995 × 6.0000 × 5.0000 mm** spanning z 1.1412 → 6.1412, a lens-barrel shape and the tallest thing on the board by 1.80 mm over the next component; the bottom is −4.7100. Those two numbers are the whole of z — 6.1412 − (−4.7100) = 10.8512, rounded up to 10.87. **Which solid is the camera is still not established** and the citation says so: geometry alone cannot tell, no silkscreen or placement file was consulted, and this is explicitly not a claim that the barrel is the OV2640. What it does buy is a falsifiable check — an OV5640 board's *overall* thickness against 10.85. Above it, z must be re-derived for that SKU; at or below it, this envelope covers both. No vendor publishes the module height: LILYGO's product page, the repo README and the resellers' listings were all checked and none states one. The envelope itself is unchanged.

### Added
- **Fourth envelope: `boards/xiao-esp32s3`, the plain module** (2026-09-11). The registry carried the Sense and not the board underneath it; Oh-Ben-Claw #151 adds the board row (USB id `0x2886:0x0056`, sourced to arduino-esp32's own board definition rather than asserted), and this is the ingest of it plus its envelope. **22.51 × 17.81 × 4.48 mm**, bounding box of Seeed's own `XIAO-ESP32S3 v2.step` (hashed, zip hashed, OCCT 7.9.3, method stated). Same axis permutation as the Sense — the model's Y is the board normal — and this time the permutation is *corroborated* rather than assumed: the model's Z extent is 17.8040 mm against Seeed's stated 17.8 mm width. Conflicts recorded, not resolved: Seeed states `21 × 17.8mm` and no height at all, the extra 1.5 mm of length being the USB-C connector overhanging the PCB outline; and the vendor's `XIAO_ESP32S3_v1.1_Dimensioning.dxf` is unusable despite its name — an R12 export with **zero** DIMENSION entities, no declared unit, and extents matching neither the outline nor the model. Nothing relates the model's "v2" to the DXF's "v1.1". 106 entries.
- **Correction to the entry below.** It said the plain XIAO's STEP measured `22.50 × 17.80 × 4.48`. Those are truncated; this repo rounds envelope axes **up** to 0.01, which makes them 22.51 and 17.81. The board also no longer "has no entry" — that sentence was true for exactly as long as the upstream registry carried only the Sense.
- **`boards/lilygo-t-camera-plus-s3`, ingested and with an envelope** (2026-09-11). The board row comes from Oh-Ben-Claw #140 (registry regenerated), so this entry is ingest output, not hand-written; the ingest touched exactly one file. Envelope **30.00 × 69.67 × 10.87 mm** from LILYGO's own V1.2 STEP (hashed, commit-pinned, OCCT 7.9.3, method stated; Z-up frame, no permutation). Revision stated: V1.2; the V1.0–V1.1 model exists and was not measured. The STEP is the only dimensional source — the schematic is electrical only and neither LILYGO's README nor product page gives dimensions — and the citation says so; the OV5640 SKU's height is flagged as unverified. 105 entries. First enclosure: OpenDesignCore run 74, Blender verification 75 agrees.
- **Third envelope: `boards/xiao-esp32s3-sense`** (2026-09-11) — bounding box of Seeed Studio's own Sense STEP model (hashed, OCCT 7.9.3, method stated), **24.38 × 17.81 × 14.02 mm** with the camera expansion board fitted. The model's frame has Y as the board normal, so the citation records the axis permutation it applied. Recorded as a conflict rather than resolved: Seeed's specification table says 15 mm tall with the expansion board; the model measures 14.01. The vendor DXF drawings are unitless R12 outlines with no dimension entities and were not used for numbers. The plain XIAO ESP32-S3 has no entry because the upstream registry carries only the Sense; its STEP (22.50 × 17.80 × 4.48 in the same frame) is on disk and hashed for when it does. First enclosure around it: OpenDesignCore run 70, Blender verification 71 agrees.
- **ADR-0006 — `envelope_mm` with its own source.** Optional top-level `envelope_mm {x, y, z, tolerance_mm?, source}`; the validator requires all three axes positive and a citation of the envelope's own, since the entry-level citation for every ingested board is a registry with no dimensions. `ingest_obc.py` now preserves `envelope_mm` and `links` across a re-ingest instead of deleting them. Rust binding hoists `envelope_mm: Option<EnvelopeMm>` (8 tests); TS binding types `PartEnvelope`. First two envelopes: `boards/dfrobot-firebeetle2-esp32s3` (bounding box of DFRobot's STEP model, hashed and method stated) and `electronic/esp32-s3-wroom-1` (Espressif datasheet; migrated from OpenDesignCore's private `data/parts/`). 104 entries.
- Repo scaffolded: schema v0, boards namespace with one ingested entry (esp32-s3, from Oh-Ben-Claw registry schema_version 1), stdlib validator, docs and ADRs.
- ADR-0003: hand-rolled binding emitters with golden-fixture parity.
- `scripts/ingest_obc.py`: idempotent ingest of Oh-Ben-Claw's registry. 69 upstream USB-identity rows become **66 board models** carrying `usb_ids` lists (ADR-0004 — neither name nor VID/PID is a key upstream), plus 34 accessories into `electronic` (ADR-0005). 100 entries, all cited, all valid.
- `scripts/emit_ts.py` + `bindings/typescript/parts.ts`: first generated binding, with a `--check` staleness gate so data changes without regenerated bindings fail rather than drift. Compiles under `tsc --strict`.
- Generic passive families: electronic/r-0603 and electronic/c-0603. Package dimensions cited to IPC-SM-782A (via the KiCad footprint that names the page); resistance, dielectric, tolerance and voltage are explicitly properties of the BOM line and of a chosen MPN, not of the family, and are marked TODO(source). Closes the empty-opc_id gap in OpenCircuitCore's BOM. 102 entries.
- `scripts/emit_rust.py` + `bindings/rust/`: the Rust binding Oh-Ben-Claw needs to consume this registry instead of carrying its own copy — the drift its own docs describe. Crate `openpartscore`, **zero dependencies**, whole registry as const data with no runtime parsing. `capabilities` and `usb_ids` are hoisted and typed; everything else stays as `attributes_json`.
- `candidates_for_usb(vid, pid)` returns an **iterator, not an Option** — the API makes ADR-0004's many-to-many mapping impossible to ignore. 7 `cargo test` cases pin it, including that `0x303a:0x1001` yields more than five candidate boards and that one board holds three identities.
- `--check` staleness gate matches the TypeScript emitter's; both bindings and the validator are green at 102 entries.
